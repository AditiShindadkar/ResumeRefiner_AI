Senior Data Analyst - Example Corp

About the role
You will own the analytics behind our subscription business, partnering with product, marketing and finance stakeholders to turn data into decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain self-serve dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Write production-quality SQL against Snowflake and model data with dbt
- Design and analyse A/B tests and communicate results to non-technical stakeholders
- Develop forecasting and churn models in Python
- Automate recurring reporting with Airflow

Requirements
- 3+ years in data analysis or data science
- Strong SQL and Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn)
- Experience with a cloud warehouse such as Snowflake or BigQuery
- Solid statistics and hypothesis testing
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Nice to have: AWS, Docker, experience with LLM-based tooling
